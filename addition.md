How does the addition of two integers work? It is a fast and straight forward process. It is the process of combining two whole numbers by using the + sign which is the sign for addition.
An example of this is 7+13=20. I took the two numbers and combined them to get the sum of the two.
Another example of this is 73+3=76. It is the same thing just with one bigger number.

The way multiplication works is similar to addition in the fact it creates a bigger sum with two numbers. It uses the "*" or "x" symbol as the operator. when two numbers are combined, you add one number the amount of times of the other number.

So, 5 * 5 is the same as 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5. The problem can also be phrased as what is 5, 5 times?

Example: 7 * 2 = 14
